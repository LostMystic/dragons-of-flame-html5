# DL2 "Dragons of Flame" (TSR 9132, 1984, Douglas Niles): what the module has that the game does not

Source: `AD&D DL - Dragons of Flame.pdf` (text extracted to `work/module/dl2rc.txt`). Area numbers are the module's own. Effort tags: **[S]** small (text, a sprite, a rule), **[M]** medium (a new NPC / room / mechanic), **[L]** large (a new area or set piece).

What the game already has from the module (so we do not repeat it): draconians (Baaz, Kapak, and the game's Bozak), gully dwarves (Aghar), Eben Shatterstone, Gilthanas (wandering, joins), Laurana (met outside and found as a captive), the sword Wyrmslayer, the freed women and children, trolls / wights / wraiths / zombies, wasps, dire wolves, griffons, two red dragons on level 4, the Sla-Mori approach, the Pax Tharkas south door, the staff of Magius and the Disks of Mishakal.

---

## 1. Death effects and monster rules (Appendix 2) — easiest wins
- **[S] Baaz turn to a metal statue, then crumble.** At 0 hp the body becomes a statue; a melee blow needs a Dexterity check at -3 or the weapon sticks and cannot be used; the statue crumbles to dust in 1-4 rounds. Only the body does this; weapons and armour it carried can be used. (We have the crumble; the *stone statue* stage and the *stuck weapon* are not in.)
- **[S] Wyrmslayer is immune to the stick.** Also: double damage against **draconians** as well as dragons; the wielder gets **+3 on saves against breath weapons and spells cast by dragons or draconians**; it **buzzes loudly within 30 ft of a true dragon and always wakes a sleeping dragon**. (The game only has double damage against dragons.)
- **[S] Kapak saliva and blades.** The Kapak lick their swords before a fight (a full round to envenom; the poison lasts 3 rounds and paralyses 2-12 turns on a failed save vs poison); the acid pool is 10 ft across and burns 1-8 damage per round to anyone standing in it, and dissolves wood and leather at 1 inch per round; their own gear is ruined. Draconians "toy with the PCs" first, stopping 180 ft away to lick the blades.
- **[S] Draconian movement.** Three speeds: walking, running on all fours with wings beating (weapon in teeth), and gliding (4 ft of glide per foot of height). They cannot truly fly for more than a round.
- **[S] Baaz in disguise** (hood and mask, wings hidden) used as spies before an invasion.
- **[S] Aghar traits.** Save as if 2 levels higher; say "do anything, no matter how mean, to survive"; think magic is a sham to be exposed; terrible health, great memories (a source of information); the cry "priwicy" (privacy).
- **[S] Troll regeneration is 3 hp per round** and trolls attack 3 times (5-8/5-8/2-12); the game has a one-hp-per-step regrowth and the "only magic kills them" message.
- **[S] Zombies "always strike last", are immune to sleep, charm, hold, cold** and fear turning only when turned; turned zombies **go back to their tombs**.
- **[S] Wraith** only hit by silver or magical weapons; energy drain.
- **[S] Giant slug** spits acid (up to 132 pts of damage in the module's typo; read as 13-ish) and cannot turn around in a corridor narrower than 20 ft.
- **[S] Kender abilities for Tasslehoff:** *Taunt* (target saves vs spells or attacks wildly for 1-10 rounds at -2 to hit and +2 to its AC) and *Fearlessness* (immune to fear). Kender are curious about everything.
- **[S] Elves:** elf warriors and the Qualinesti are ranged and graceful; half-elves (Tanis) are treated with suspicion by Qualinesti elves.

## 2. Items and treasure
- **[S] Nightbringer**, Verminaard's +3 footman's mace: on a hit, the command word "Midnight" blinds the victim 2-12 turns (-4 to attacks, no Dex or shield bonus) unless they save; a good character who picks it up must save at -2 or is blinded permanently (until cure blindness).
- **[S] Verminaard's plate mail +2**, shiny blue; and his dragonlord mask (horns, machine-like visor).
- **[S] Staff of Magius:** +3 protection, +2 to hit (dmg 1-8), continual light once a day, feather fall once a day.
- **[S] Tasslehoff's hoopak** is a bullet sling (2-5/2-7) *and* a +2 jo stick (1-6+2).
- **[S] Goldmoon's Medallion of Faith**, which **magically duplicates** when a new true cleric appears, bearing that god's sign (Paladine for Elistan). Perfect for the Elistan scene.
- **[S] Quith-pa:** the elven dried fruit used as iron rations (two weeks' worth packed for the party).
- **[S] Steel (stl)** is the money of Krynn; gold is nearly worthless (see the 25,000 gold ingots).
- **[S] Troll lair loot:** a shield +1, a locked strongbox with 257 sp and gems worth 150, 100 and 200 stl, a rusty key.
- **[S] Verminaard's hutch:** 2 potions of extra healing, a potion of gaseous form, a potion of invisibility, guarded by a **poison needle** (save or die). **His desk:** maps of Ansalon and of Qualinesti **pierced by three great daggers** (the invasion plan), a diagram of the gate-blocking mechanism, and a drawer trapped with **sleep gas**; it holds two clerical scrolls (cure serious wounds / prayer / find traps; and light / augury / cure light wounds). His closet holds a black cape, boots and black plate mail.
- **[S] Armoury leftovers:** 23 shortswords, 8 longswords, 108 spear heads (no shafts), 60 daggers, all rusty.
- **[S] Guardroom loot:** 54 stl, 187 sp, 327 bp, a key to the prison cell; a hobgoblin with a ruby worth 300.

## 3. Named characters the game never shows
- **[M] Tika Waylan** (frying-pan fighter, ex-thief, afraid of heights, fascinated by magic; hits for double damage with her pan; gold ring on a chain). Scene: Otik's spiced potatoes at the ruined **Inn of the Last Home**, "Sit down! Git somethin' to eat — you look like a pack of starved rats."
- **[M] Theros Ironfeld**, the human smith of Solace who smuggled elves out and lost his right arm; healing him is a "miracle" the soldiers witness.
- **[M] Fewmaster Toede** (ugly hobgoblin, wispy white hair, toad-like, bully, coward, can leap 30 ft; rides a pony, later a wyvern) and his servant **Sestun**, the gully dwarf with the huge red nose and oversized helmet who shatters the cage locks and frees the party. Toede's "speech" to Verminaard, his whining, his threats to the prisoners.
- **[M] Porthios** (the elven war leader), **the Speaker of Suns** (father of Gilthanas, Porthios and Laurana; aloof, cool to Tanis), the **court poet** who recites the Canticle.
- **[M] Elistan** (high priest of Haven; at first a disbeliever, injured in the mines; when healed he embraces Paladine, and the medallion duplicates).
- **[M] Maritta the seamstress**, leader of the women, who leads ten women nightly to feed the children; the children's own leaders (a mature boy and girl).
- **[M] Highklahd Drooth**, chief of the Klahd Clan of the Aghar, who trips over his sword and loses his tin helmet.
- **[M] Verminaard, Dragonlord of the Red Wing** (8th-level cleric, spells cause fear, cause disease, spiritual hammer, prayer, snake charm, animate dead...). Rides **Ember (Pyros)**, the huge red dragon (spells: sleep, web, mirror image, haste, slow, polymorph self, wall of fire). The game has dragons but never names them or Verminaard.
- **[M] Flamestrike (Mataflure)**, the half-blind scarred mother dragon who lost her brood and now guards the children; will never breathe fire near them.
- **[S] Kitiara** is mentioned in the prologue as the beautiful one who did not return.

## 4. Story set pieces (the journey before Sla-Mori)
- **[L] Que Kiri** burned town: the blinded old man ("What shall become of my garden?"), nine Kapak hiding in the rubble; the old man's last words as a callback line.
- **[L] Solace**, the tree-city razed (stakes in the square, a pole fence, vallenwoods burned, the Inn sitting on the ground), patrols that ignore you unless you draw weapons; a battle leads to 100 reinforcements and capture.
- **[L] "Captured!"**: two red dragons with Kapak riders ("Drop your weapons, little mortals, or you shall feel the heat of my pet in anger") whenever the party dawdles in the open.
- **[L] The slave caravan**: three caged wagons pulled by elk, 45 Kapak ahead, 100 goblins, 100 hobgoblins behind, goods wagon with the party's confiscated gear (Toede claims items). Nothing to eat or drink for a day and a night. Gilthanas' tale of Kith-Kanan, the Kinslayer War, Silvanos, the Swordsheath Scroll, and the building of Pax Tharkas as a symbol of peace between elves and dwarves.
- **[L] "Fight for Freedom"**: elven bird-calls in the forest, arrows from the dark, Sestun smashing the locks, goblins arriving two per round, the party leaving the cage two per round.
- **[L] Qualinost**: the hidden path behind the waterfall, the footbridge over the chasm, the silver-lined towers and quartz avenues, the golden tower, a child shrieking at the sight of a dwarf, the **Speaker of Suns' council**, the **mosaic map** on the cover, and Laurana leading the party to the aspen grove with fruit trees. Elves flee west.
- **[M] Laurana as a spoiled princess** (betrothed to Tanis as a child, a crush on any male Qualinesti elf; chain mail +1, AC 0). Her growth is the module's arc.
- **[M] "Kidnap!"**: a wyvern ridden by Toede lands in Qualinost, six Kapak as a diversion ("Your loyalty is touching, my little Kapaks!"), and Laurana is taken. (The game finds her captive in Sla-Mori; this is the missing "how".)
- **[M] The prophetic dream** if the heroes refuse the elves: Qualinesti burns, seven draconians (three licking swords) kill you. The dream "comes true to the letter".
- **[M] Eben's staged ambush** (22): twelve Baaz attack, Eben "wounded" with blood on his legs but refusing healing — he is Verminaard's plant to betray the party. The game has Eben but not the betrayal.
- **[M] The hidden valley and the trolls** (24): three trolls take two heroes to their lair; the glowing gem Gilthanas uses to open the Sla-Mori gate with a chant (25).

## 5. Sla-Mori: rooms and traps not in the game
- **[M] Crossroads of tracks** (humanoid and small clawed): the dust shows where traffic goes; the gate corridor is untracked.
- **[L] Hall of the Ancients (Kith-Kanan's tomb)** (28): huge columns fallen like felled vallenwoods, a granite throne between two statues three times man-height with granite swords, the skeleton of the founder with a worn regal cloak and **Wyrmslayer across his lap**, bronze doors with a relief of Pax Tharkas, thousands of timid rats in the rubble.
- **[M] Giant Slug corridor** (29): a 60-ft wide corridor with a dust-free path; the slug sleeps wedged in the cave-in.
- **[M] Chamber of Doom** (30): the wraith is the remains of a grave-robber who got trapped by the magic gate.
- **[M] The Sliding Wall** (31): a loop corridor; stepping on a sinking floor plate closes a wall (needs combined Strength 50 to push, up to 5 characters; cannot be opened from the far side).
- **[L] Tomb of the Zombies** (32): 40 zombies on stone biers, two behind each door of a long hall, the elite guard of Kith-Kanan corrupted to kill all intruders; they rise when a door opens, **any magic is used** (except lit weapons), the south doors open, or the wraith commands them.
- **[S] Hall of Columns** (33): untouched dust, no encounters for a long stretch (a quiet room).
- **[M] The pit trap corridor** (34): dwarven engineers rigged a false door; pressure drops a 20-ft floor section into a pit, 3d6 damage.
- **[L] The Chain and Support room** (35): a huge slanting chain with man-long links, a foot-thick iron bracket in the floor. Only a kender or child can climb it (Climb roll) to reach Pax Tharkas's defence chamber.
- **[S] Tharkadan Treasure Vault** (36): behind a secret door with a latch, 25,000 gold ingots (1,000 gp each) stacked 25 high — and useless in Krynn.
- **[S] The secret door to Pax Tharkas** (37) beside a small catch; the cellar (38) has a **well** (first of the besieged fortress's wells) and crates from Solace and Gateway (a Wisdom check recognises them).

## 6. Pax Tharkas: rooms, scenes and mechanics
- **[M] Lower Guardroom** (39): a draconian drags Laurana toward Verminaard ("My lord Verminaard requires your presence! Who are you to refuse his noble calling?"); eight Kapak; the keys to all the prison rooms are on a guard's belt; if the party is late, Laurana dies an "obscure death".
- **[M] The alarm ropes** (area 63): pull one and the fortress is alerted: **double the random encounter checks and the number of creatures; surprise impossible**.
- **[M] The three women's prisons**: 34 women (Maritta and the injured Elistan), 60 mothers, 45 maidens; 287 more in the large prison (66) in the west tower guarded by 8 hobgoblins. The women explain: the dragon, the children, and that **armed men disguised in women's shawls can pass as the feeding parties** (10 women go to feed the children each evening, 12 feed the miners).
- **[M] Chamber of the Aghar** (43): the see-saw catapult game with stones and a tall box, tin helmets, swords longer than their legs; the Aghar can be **bribed with toys, rope, food, weapons** and know how the stone-drop works; they are friendlier if **Sestun** is rescued.
- **[S] Storage room** (44): 72 barrels of oil etc.; burning it fills the corridor with smoke (1-6 damage per round) for twenty turns.
- **[S] Upper Hallway** (45): plush purple carpet, stained and muddy; tapestries defaced with charcoal (a beard on an elven princess, slashes through an elf/dwarf council). Two hobgoblin guards demand identification.
- **[M] Reception room** (46): five drunken Kapak around a keg who run when three are killed and raise the alarm.
- **[M] The Dining Room** (47): untouched, a table laid for four with a platinum pitcher and goblets of **ice water**, candles with no dripped wax (lit just now) — a clue.
- **[M] The Kitchen** (48): four Aghar cooks wading in gravy and cooking for Verminaard, who plans to entertain **a woman from downstairs**; the gully dwarves become friendlier if you are the Dragonlord's enemies, and know Sestun is imprisoned.
- **[M] Hobgoblin Guardroom** (49): 28 hobgoblins gambling; a key to Sestun's cell; fighting with the door closed does not raise the alarm.
- **[M] Sestun's cell** (50): "the gully dwarf who freed the heroes"; any captured PC is imprisoned here and their gear goes behind Verminaard's throne; **refusing to betray the party gets you fed to Ember**.
- **[L] Verminaard's Throne Room** (51): mirrored walls, the scene of **Toede being scolded** ("Toede, you miserable rodent, you have the gift of ruining the simplest plans!"), Verminaard sees eavesdroppers in the mirrors and calls "Ember!"; if Ember is hurt to half she flees up the chimney after Verminaard mounts.
- **[M] Verminaard's Quarters** (52a-c): waiting room tapestry of a red dragon burning a village, private dining with the poison-needle hutch, bedroom with bearskins, jaguar, panther, wolf and tiger rugs and the invasion maps.
- **[L] Children's Playroom and Flamestrike's chamber** (53-57): a barred door, toy dolls and rag balls, an arch into the dark, the great red dragon asleep (one good eye, blunted teeth, long scars, scrawny); 180 children ("not even the smallest baby is crying"); the children refuse to leave unless convinced their parents will follow; Flamestrike wakes when most have passed ("Maritta, you take all of my children together... is it not easier with a few at a time?") and will not use her breath near the children.
- **[M] Upper Landing and the secret door** (58): silver-coated doors with an elf and a dwarf holding a **lute** (the ancient symbol of peace); a twisting stone opens the secret door.
- **[L] The Gallery** (59): a curved room of paintings showing the history of Pax Tharkas — the dwarf-and-elf builders, dragons raging, silver lances driving them back (the Dragonlance legend), defaced portraits, ashes of burned paintings. Pure lore for a callback.
- **[M] The Balcony** (60) overlooking the throne room pit and a second dark pit with a resting red dragon.
- **[L] Chain chamber** (61), **Gate-blocking mechanism** (62): a narrow walkway through the Tharkadan wall, a winch (combined Strength 40, 3-18 rounds) that drops granite blocks, **killing everyone in area 63**; Ember's fire through the crack heats the chain red then white until it snaps and **seals the gates for a month**. If the stone drop is used the pursuing army is delayed 4 days (otherwise 12 hours).
- **[S] The Great Wall (63):** winches need Strength 80, two turns; the north gate is cracked open (6 ft), the south shut.
- **[M] Upper barracks** (floors 2-5): 1-20 Kapak left behind on each upper floor.
- **[M] Monster Mess Hall** (67): 7 Baaz and 6 hobgoblins in a smoky yellow haze; **Western Supply Room** (68) of leather armour and crates of "mysterious smoked meat (wild dog)"; **Western Kitchen** (69) with the **second well**; **Armoury** (70).
- **[L] The Tharkadan Mines** (71-73): the **smelter with unsupervised Aghar** hammering red rock and stoking vats in sulphurous smoke; the **slave quarters** cave; the iron mine with 310 men, dozens of lightly-guarded draconians; slaves **will not join any risky act without assurance their families are safe**; when they see their families they turn on the guards; Elistan organises the exodus.
- **[L] "Escape from Pax Tharkas"** (finale): the doors burst, Flamestrike emerges ("My children! You shall not have my children!"), Ember lands on the wall with Verminaard ("Now, I destroy you! I destroy your wives! I destroy your children!"), and **Flamestrike breathes fire on Verminaard** and the two dragons fight while about 800 people escape down the valley.
- **[M] Epilogue camp**: the escape ends in a pine grove with 800 people; **skilled teachers among the rescued give every fighter, magic-user and thief a free level**; clerics gain a level by meditation (Mishakal).

## 7. Random encounters (Appendix 1)
Frequency tables for town ruins, plains, mountains, Sla-Mori and Pax Tharkas; encounters on a 1 in 10 roll. Flavour entries we do not have:
- **[S] Wild dogs** (weak, hungry, **friendly if fed**), **goblins**, **giant rams** (only one is a ram, the rest ewes — he fights, they flee), **giant centipedes** (poison save at -4), **hobgoblins** (retreat at half dead), **a wyvern that carries off an NPC**, **elk** (3-30, harmless), **carrion crawlers** (paralysis), **griffons desperate for food** (attack a horse or PC at random; feed them and they run), **war dogs**, **townsmen** (not hostile, flee if attacked), **Aghar** that bully you and run at the first wound.
- **[S] Kapak patrols come one per game hour** if the party strays, "an infinite supply" — the module's pressure to stay on the path.

## 8. Lore, voice and presentation callbacks
- **[S] "The Iconochronos of Astinus of Palanthus"** prologue narrator: Astinus the Lorekeeper "rises from the crystal globe of wisdom, weary from following the trails of history". The intro could open in his voice ("Here ends this chapter of the Iconochronos.").
- **[S] Mishakal's message**: "Your journey is not complete; you must leave here, must search for a true Leader of the People."
- **[S] The Canticle of the Dragon** (Michael Williams; Appendix 4): the poem of Huma, the Cataclysm, the Age of Despair, the Innfellows and the Sunken City. It fits the ending slide show or the credits.
- **[S] The song "Elvenhome"** (Tracy Hickman and Michael Williams, arr. Frank Mentzer), sung by the Qualinesti after the council.
- **[S] The Cataclysm / Kingpriest / Istar**, the Age of Dreams, the Kinslayer War, the Swordsheath Scroll, Silvanos and Kith-Kanan: all good short texts for books, signs or the history gallery.
- **[S] The twins' quote and character notes** on the Player Character cards: each hero has a first-person paragraph (Tanis on Laurana vs Kitiara; Sturm "Perunde cadaver — Obedience Unto Death"; Flint on gully dwarves not being hill dwarves, wanting more time in Xak Tsaroth; Tasslehoff "Even gully dwarves and Seekers may look like long-lost friends"; Riverwind "I would wage war against the destroyers of my homeland"). Great hero barks when freeing prisoners, entering the Sla-Mori, meeting an Aghar, etc.
- **[S] Obscure deaths:** named enemies (Toede, Eben, Verminaard) should vanish rather than leave bodies, so they can return.
- **[S] Characteristic Checks** (d20 at or under the ability): used for sticking weapons, noticing the Solace crates, resisting the alarm.
- **[S] Hero stats:** Tanis half-elf F6, Caramon F7, Sturm F7, Raistlin MU4 (spellbook includes Tenser's floating disc, push, hold portal, knock, wizard lock, ESP, mirror image, audible glamer, darkness), Goldmoon C6 (3/3/2 spells), Riverwind R6, Flint F5, Tasslehoff T5 (pockets, locks, traps...). Raistlin's pupils are hourglass-shaped and his skin golden.

## 9. Suggested order of work (cheapest and most noticeable first)
1. **Death rules and Wyrmslayer buzz** (section 1): stone-statue stage and stuck weapon for Baaz; Wyrmslayer buzzes near dragons and doubles vs draconians.
2. **Name the two dragons** Ember and Flamestrike, give Flamestrike the "never breathes near the children" rule, and add the **mirror throne room / Verminaard** as the second dragon's boss scene.
3. **Hero barks** from the character cards when a prisoner is freed, an Aghar appears, a dragon appears.
4. **Elistan** at the women's prison: a healing scene with the medallion duplicating, and Maritta leading ten women.
5. **Tika** and **Sestun** as joinable/helpful NPCs; Aghar clans that can be bribed with toys; the Aghar **see-saw room**.
6. **Gallery of paintings** and **Kith-Kanan's tomb** (Hall of the Ancients) with the throne, skeleton and statues in Sla-Mori.
7. **Gate-blocking chain**: a lever room that seals the gate and delays the armies; a cut-scene where Ember's fire snaps the chain.
8. **Escape from Pax Tharkas finale**: Flamestrike vs Ember and Verminaard on the wall while the 800 escape; epilogue camp with free level-ups.
9. **Opening chapters** (Que Kiri witness, Solace, caravan, Qualinost council, Laurana's kidnapping) as short illustrated scenes or text slides in the intro.
